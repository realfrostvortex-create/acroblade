Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Acroblade is a free game. This page explains what the game and this website do with information. We keep it simple: there are no accounts, no ads and no analytics in the game.

What stays on your device

Your settings and progress (such as level, XP and unlocks) are saved on your own device, in the app's local storage. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing site data removes them.

Downloads and this website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and the Windows installer is hosted on GitHub Releases. GitHub may record technical data such as your IP address when you visit or download, under GitHub's privacy statement. We do not receive or add tracking to this site.

Third-party services the game may contact

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com): the game may load fonts from Google, which can see your IP address.
  • Cloudflare cdnjs: the online menu loads the PeerJS library from cdnjs when you use online play.

Online play

Online play is not available worldwide yet. When it is, it uses peer-to-peer connections (WebRTC) and a matchmaking service. Other players in the same room may be able to see your IP address, and the matchmaking service handles the room details needed to connect players. Do not enter personal information in player names or room codes.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will change the date above. Questions: open an issue at github.com/realfrostvortex-create/acroblade/issues.
