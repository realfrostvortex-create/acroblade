About Acroblade

Acroblade is a free grapple-and-sword action game for Windows. Swing with twin grapple hooks, slash enemy bots, chain combos for a higher score, and race the clock in BLITZ mode, a 90-second score attack.

  • Fast movement: swing, glide and fight in 3D arenas
  • Bot battles with several weapons
  • Online matches for up to 4 players (coming soon)
  • Touch controls with gyro aiming for phones
